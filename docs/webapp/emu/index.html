BoopBox emulator

The real BoopBox firmware, running in your browser: the same screen, sequencer and sound engines as on the M-VAVE FM-1. Try it before you install, or work on a beat away from the device.

Sound needs a click to start. Best with headphones.

Your projects, songs and settings are kept in this browser.
Keys: click them (lower on a key plays harder, with MENU › KEYS › VELOCITY on), or Z S X … ' from F3 and Q W 3 … Y up to G5.
Buttons: hold them for their layer. Shift-click keeps one held, so the keyboard can play its layer. Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, [ ] OCT− OCT+.
Knobs: drag up or down, or scroll. ↑ ↓ SELECT, ← → ALGORITHM, PgUp PgDn PRESETS.
More buttons: 1 FX, 2 SEL, 5 ENV, 7 LFO, 8 EDIT, 9 GLO, 0 SAVE, - ARP, = SEQ.

The panel is drawn, not photographed: the controls are the FM-1's, their places close to it. Not here: USB and MIDI, USB audio, the web editor's link, your own samples, the keys' own velocity sensing. After JIANT FM's emulator. Install BoopBox on your FM-1 · GPL-3.0.
